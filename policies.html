Return Policy

We want you to be happy with your purchase. If something isn't right, we're here to help.

Note: The exact return period, conditions, and exclusions are still to be confirmed by the client. The drafts below use common defaults for review.

Eligibility for Returns

To be eligible for a return, your item must be:

  • Unused and in the same condition that you received it
  • In the original packaging with tags intact (where applicable)
  • Returned within [RETURN PERIOD — e.g. 7 days] of delivery TBC
  • Accompanied by the original receipt or proof of purchase

Non-Returnable Items

Certain products cannot be returned for hygiene, safety, or customisation reasons:

  • Beauty and personal care products (e.g. lotions, soaps, lip products) that have been opened
  • Gift cards
  • Customised or personalised items
  • Items marked as "Final Sale" or "Non-returnable" on the product page

How to Initiate a Return

  1. Contact us via WhatsApp or email with your order number and the item you'd like to return
  2. We'll review your request and confirm eligibility within 1 business day
  3. If approved, we'll share return instructions and the return address (within Benin City)
  4. Package the item securely with all original packaging
  5. Send the package — return shipping costs are the customer's responsibility unless the item was damaged or incorrect
  6. We'll inspect the returned item and process your refund or exchange within 3–5 business days of receipt

Refunds

Once your return is received and inspected, we'll notify you by email or WhatsApp. If approved, your refund will be processed to your original payment method within 5–7 business days. Refund timing depends on your bank or payment provider.

Damaged or Incorrect Items

If you receive a damaged, defective, or incorrect item, please contact us within 48 hours of delivery with photos. We'll arrange a free replacement or full refund — no return shipping cost to you.

Exchanges

We're happy to exchange items for a different size, color, or variant (subject to availability). The fastest way to exchange is to return the original item and place a new order for the desired item.

Orders are processed within 24 hours of placement (and after payment confirmation for bank transfer orders). Orders placed on weekends or public holidays are processed on the next business day.

Every order moves through these statuses — you'll receive email updates at each stage:

  3. Shipped / Out for Delivery — your order is on the way
  4. Delivered — your order has arrived

Failed Deliveries

If a delivery attempt is unsuccessful (e.g. no one available to receive), we'll contact you to reschedule. A second delivery fee may apply for repeated failed attempts.

Order Cancellation

Plans change — here's how to cancel an order.

You can request cancellation of an order before it has been processed or shipped. Contact us via WhatsApp or email with your order number as soon as possible. If we cancel before processing, we'll issue a full refund within 5–7 business days.

Once an order has been processed or shipped, it cannot be cancelled. You can still return eligible items after delivery — see the Return Policy above.
