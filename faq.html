Payment

We accept online payments (cards, USSD, bank transfer via our payment provider) and direct bank transfers. For bank transfers, you'll receive our account details after checkout and will need to send proof of payment to confirm your order.

Yes — our website uses SSL encryption (HTTPS) and our payment is processed by a PCI-compliant provider. We never see or store your card details. PROVIDER TBC

Choose "Bank Transfer" at checkout, place your order, then transfer the total to the bank account shown on the confirmation page (also sent by email). Send your proof of payment via WhatsApp or email with your order number. We'll process your order once payment is confirmed (usually within 24 hours).

All prices are in Nigerian Naira (₦). We currently only accept Naira payments.

Returns & Refunds

Certain products are eligible for returns within the return period (TBC). Items must be unused, in original packaging, and accompanied by proof of purchase. Some products (e.g. opened beauty items) are non-returnable for hygiene reasons. See our return policy for full details.

Once we receive and inspect your returned item, we'll process your refund to the original payment method within 3–5 business days. Bank processing time may add another 2–3 business days.

Please contact us within 48 hours of delivery with photos of the damaged or incorrect item. We'll arrange a free replacement or full refund — no return shipping cost to you.
