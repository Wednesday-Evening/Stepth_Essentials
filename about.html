About Stepth Essentials

Your Essentials, Your Way.

We're a friendly, modern online variety store based in Benin City, Nigeria — offering a curated range of useful products for everyday needs, while building lasting relationships with the people who shop with us.

Our Mission

To provide a convenient and trusted online shopping experience, offering a variety of useful products while building lasting relationships with customers.

Steph Essentials was founded by Stephnora Odion, a Nigerian entrepreneur with a passion for making everyday shopping simpler, friendlier, and more reliable. After years of seeing how difficult it could be to find quality products at fair prices — without long waits or uncertain service — she decided to build something different.

Our Customers

We serve shoppers across Benin City — and we're growing. Whether you're a busy professional looking for everyday essentials, a student setting up your first apartment, or someone hunting for a thoughtful gift, we want Stepth Essentials to be the store you come back to. Our customers value quality, fair prices, fast delivery, and being treated like real people — not order numbers. That's what we promise to deliver, every single time.

Our Products

Stepth Essentials offers a carefully chosen range across five main categories: Fashion, Beauty, Home, Electronics, and Accessories. We focus on products that are genuinely useful — things you'll reach for again and again — rather than chasing trends for their own sake. Every product we list has been selected with quality, value, and longevity in mind.

Many of our products come with variations (size, color, material, style) so you can find the right fit for your needs. We use a simple SKU system to keep things organised, and we're transparent about stock — you'll always see "In Stock", "Low Stock", or "Out of Stock" before you commit to a purchase.

Our Future Vision

Steph Essentials is starting in Benin City, but we're dreaming bigger. Over the coming months and years, we plan to:

  • Expand our delivery coverage to all major Nigerian cities
  • Grow our product range with even more carefully chosen essentials
  • Introduce customer accounts with order history and saved wishlists
  • Build a community of returning customers through honest reviews, helpful content, and personal service
  • Partner with trusted delivery and payment providers to make every transaction smoother

This is just the beginning. We're grateful for every customer who shops with us — and we'd love for you to be part of the journey.
